Clinical Trials in India (2001-2026) Power BI Analytics
Overview:
This project analyzes clinical trials conducted in India from 2001 to Q1 2026, focusing on study characteristics, intervention categories, participant demographics, and geographic distribution.
Key Questions:
1. How are clinical trials distributed across study phases?
2. What proportion of trials are interventional vs observational?
3. Which intervention categories are most represented?
4. How have intervention categories changed over time?
5. What participant age groups are represented?
6. How are trials distributed by participant sex?
7. How does trial activity vary across Indian states?
